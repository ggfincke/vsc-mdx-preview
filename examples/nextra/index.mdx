# Nextra MDX Preview Examples

This example demonstrates **Nextra framework support** in MDX Preview. All Nextra components are supported out of the box.

---

## Callouts

Callouts are alert boxes with 5 built-in types:

<Callout type="default">
  **Default** - General tips and information with a lightbulb icon.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  **Info** - Informational notes with a circle-i icon.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  **Warning** - Cautionary messages with a triangle icon.
</Callout>

<Callout type="error">
  **Error** - Critical errors with a diamond icon.
</Callout>

<Callout type="important">
  **Important** - Crucial information with a message icon.
</Callout>
